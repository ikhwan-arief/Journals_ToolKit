Every item on this page comes from two official DOAJ sources and nothing else. Use it to prepare your answers and check your journal website before applying. DOAJ only accepts applications online at doaj.org/apply, not by spreadsheet or PDF, and always makes its own independent decision.

Part A – Application form

The same questions, in the same order, as the DOAJ online application form. Help text is DOAJ's own. Links must point to the exact page on your journal website where the information is shown.

Part B – Principles of transparency & best practice

DOAJ reviews applications against these principles. Answer from what is publicly visible on your journal website and in your published articles. If you cannot point to it, the honest answer is No. N/A is offered only where the principle is conditional ("if…").

How to read the result:
• Not eligible as answered: an answer conflicts with a condition of the application form (e.g. not open access, editorial review only for a non-arts & humanities journal, invalid ISSN).
• Incomplete: required form fields or principle checks are still unanswered.
• Transparency gaps: the form can be filled in, but at least one principle is not met on your website. Fix these before applying.
• Ready to apply: every required form field is complete and every applicable principle is met. Best-practice items (Part A section 6, embedded licences, plagiarism screening, submission/acceptance dates) are encouraged but not mandatory.
