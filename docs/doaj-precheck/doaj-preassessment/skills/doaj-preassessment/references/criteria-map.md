# Frozen criterion map — rulebook 2026.10.0

This is a compact, human-readable derivative of the stakeholder
DOAJ-PreAssessment-Rulebook.md. It is the packaged assessment reference for
plugin 0.1.0, together with baseline.md and form-map.md. It does not make
the plugin an official DOAJ service or an executable rule engine.

## How to use the map

- **must** and **basic** mean required in the frozen Guide baseline.
- **should**, **best_practice**, and **principle** are guidance and never
  become automatic eligibility blockers.
- **warning_only** identifies a record to check, not a failed criterion.
- A page actually read, with a short supporting quote, can support an
  observed match or conflict. Limited search cannot prove that a page or
  policy does not exist. Use Unverifiable when coverage is incomplete.
- Statements about **all** articles, editions, people, or language
  versions need evidence across that full set. A sample supports only a
  sample observation.
- The source column names the section of the frozen Guide or Principles.
  Use the official reference URLs in baseline.md. Suggested actions
  paraphrase the stakeholder rulebook; they are not policy text to paste
  onto the journal site.

The rule IDs below belong to the proposal rulebook. Keep its requirement
priority separate from each DOAJ criterion level.

## Conditional modules

Activate a module only when the journal type is supported by observed
evidence. If the type cannot be determined, mark applicability unverifiable.
These IDs and levels follow rulebook §7. The source is the matching subsection
of the frozen Guide's Additional criteria for some journal types.

| Module and ID | Level | What to examine and suggest |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Special issues — SI-01 | must | Establish Editor-in-Chief responsibility and show that special issues remain within scope. |
| Special issues — SI-02 | must | Apply the same oversight and external peer review as regular articles, and label special issues clearly. |
| Special issues — SI-03 | must | Check and approve guest-editor credentials; an internal check may remain For DOAJ review. |
| Special issues — SI-04 | must | Show Editor-in-Chief or board oversight of guest editors. |
| Special issues — SI-05 | must | Independently review guest-editor submissions and keep them at or below 25% of the issue. A complete issue count is needed. |
| Arts and humanities — AH-01 | must | If editorial review is used, establish that at least two editors conduct it. |
| Clinical case reports — CASE-01 | must | Publish a consent policy and state in articles whether consent was given. |
| Clinical case reports — CASE-02 | must | Publish a policy for identifiable participant information and anonymisation. |
| Clinical case reports — CASE-03 | must | Provide reviewer guidelines specific to case reports. |
| Clinical case reports — CASE-04 | should | Identify case reports clearly in titles and consider CARE guidance. |
| Conference proceedings — CONF-01 | must | Confirm an ISSN and permanent board providing editorial oversight. |
| Conference proceedings — CONF-02 | must | Apply DOAJ-level peer review and provide full text for all conference papers. |
| Data journals — DATA-01 | must | Publish research articles about data rather than only dataset links or notices. |
| Overlay journals — OVL-01 | must | Select and peer review articles hosted on another site. |
| Student-run journals — STU-01 | must | Establish an advisory board with at least two PhD or equivalent members. |
| Flipped journals — FLIP-01 | must | Display the flip date plus archive availability and reuse rights. |
| Flipped journals — FLIP-02 | must | Make all content published after the flip fully OA. |
| Flipped journals — FLIP-03 | must | Show more than a year or at least ten research articles published as fully OA. |
| Mirror journals — MIR-01 | must | Use a different ISSN from the subscription title and establish at least half the same board members. |
| Subscribe to Open — S2O-01 | must | Show that the current year's target was met and that a new issue or at least five OA research articles appeared under S2O. |
| Subscribe to Open — S2O-02 | must | State the transition year and current year's OA status. |
| Subscribe to Open — S2O-03 | must | State earlier content access, reuse, and copyright terms. |
| Subscribe to Open — S2O-04 | must | Keep an opened year open permanently; report uncertainty about unpublished target or decision processes. |
| Book-review journals — BOOK-01 | must | Journals made up only of book reviews are outside this inclusion route. |

Numeric module checks require complete denominators and reliable person
identification. Guest-editor credentials, S2O targets, and other internal
practices may remain For DOAJ review when the public site cannot establish
them.

## Known unresolved decisions

Use baseline.md for the full TBD list. In particular, do not turn a measured
homepage click depth into a pass/fail threshold; do not apply the flipped
journal time threshold to every licensing change; do not claim that a small
article sample proves the endogeny percentage; and do not invent a registry
confirmation when a registry could not be accessed.
