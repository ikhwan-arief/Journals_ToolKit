# Application form map — reference PDF v3.1 (January 2026)

Use this order for the form guide. The PDF is for reference; the editor
completes the official online application at https://doaj.org/apply/.
Field names below are concise labels for the PDF's questions, not a
replacement form. The FQ IDs refer to grouped extraction rules in the
stakeholder rulebook. Expand each group into its individual official
fields as shown here.

For every field, give only the answer the observed website or accessible
registry evidence supports. If that evidence was not found or could not
be read, say **Not found on the pages inspected** or **Unverifiable**.
Never assume a No from limited browsing. A URL requested by the form
must point to the actual public page, not merely to the homepage.

## Internal checks that are not official form questions

- **FQ-19:** Detect special issues and special journal types to decide
  which conditional Guide sections may apply.
- **FQ-20:** Extract editor, board, and reviewer identities for observed
  editorial and endogeny checks.

Do not print FQ-19 or FQ-20 as application fields. Likewise, the extra
self-check prompts in the local application-form.html prototype (such as
title matching, ISSN confirmation, and an arts-and-humanities follow-up)
are preparation checks, not additional questions in the v3.1 PDF.

## Form option reminders

The v3.1 PDF lists Creative Commons options, CC0, public domain, and
publisher's own licence; peer-review types; named archiving services;
repository-policy directories; and PID types. When exact choices matter,
use the frozen PDF's options as recorded in the source rulebook. Do not
convert an observed policy into a stronger option than the site supports.
